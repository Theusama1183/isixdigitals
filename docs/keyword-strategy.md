# Keyword strategy — iSixDigitals

Source: SE Ranking MCP data (US database, October 2026). Site currently has no
organic footprint, so every term below is a genuine opportunity rather than a
defend-and-improve case.

## What the SERPs actually look like

Three of the terms that look most attractive on volume are dominated by website
builders and inspiration listicles, not by studios or designers:

| Keyword | Volume | KD | Who ranks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| photographer portfolio website | 320 | 37 | Adobe Portfolio, Pixieset, SiteBuilderReport, Reddit, Awwwards |
| photography website design | 210 | 31 | Pinterest, Pixieset, Canva, Awwwards, Figma, Flothemes |
| videographer website | 110 | 14 | Reddit, Flothemes, OnePageLove, Behance, Dribbble, Webflow |

A brand-new service page will not outrank Adobe or Canva on those queries. They
are still worth targeting, but through **examples content** that links to the
service pages rather than through the service pages alone.

The SERPs that a small studio *can* win are the niche vertical queries. On
`dance studio website design` (KD 12) the top results are independent studio
designers — `dancestudioswebdesign.com`, `sitefit.com`,
`resourcefuldance.com`, `mybeststudio.com` — with no builder or media brand in
the top eight. `sitefit.com` proves the model: it ranks **#1 and #2** for
`martial arts website design` (110 vol, KD 13, CPC $5) with a single vertical
landing page.

## Page architecture

Current structure is `/studios` as one page covering all three verticals. Split
it into three landing pages that each own one cluster:

```
/studios                      hub, links to the three below
/studios/dance                dance studios & schools
/studios/film                 film, video & photography
/studios/music-art            music, art & creative spaces
/notes/...                    examples & how-to guides (Tier 2)
```

Rules that keep this from becoming doorway pages:

- Each vertical page needs genuinely different content: the visitor on
  `/studios/dance` cares about class schedules, trial bookings and parent
  inquiries; the visitor on `/studios/film` cares about reels, credits and
  project inquiries. Reusing the same copy with the noun swapped will not rank.
- The `/notes` articles must be real editorial value — annotated examples with
  screenshots and reasoning — because they compete with Awwwards and Canva.
- `/studios` stays the hub and keeps its current content; it is not a duplicate.

## Notes on this data

- Pakistan-database volumes for every term here are ~0. The addressable market
  is US/UK English search, which matches the existing site copy.
- UK volumes are roughly 5–10× smaller than US for the same terms. Target US
  first; the same pages pick up UK rankings incidentally.
- `isixdigitals.com` returns no organic keywords in SE Ranking yet — expected
  for a newly launched domain.
- A site-wide `studio website design` (390 vol, KD 37) push is not worth it: the
  related-keyword set is dominated by New York agency-local queries with
  entirely different intent.
